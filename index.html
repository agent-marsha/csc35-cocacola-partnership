Project A · Human-state personalization

The responsive pour.

What we build

Dispensing that reads engagement and arousal from non-invasive EEG — never identity — and adapts the Share-a-Coke moment in real time, inside a consent-first architecture.

Why it matters to Coca-Cola

Personalization past purchase history to how the person actually feels — without covert sensing or stored profiles.

Project B · Accessible BCI control

The hands-free pour.

What we build

A brain- and body-controlled interface — attention, gaze and physiological signals — that selects and pours without touching the machine, on a low-cost sensing stack.

Why it matters to Coca-Cola

Serves motor-impaired and aging customers, sterile settings, and anyone whose hands are full. Accessible beverage control as a first principle, not a retrofit.

Universal design method, proven in public elections, applied to the dispenser.

Project C · Robot hosts at live venues

The living lab.

What we build

Embodied hosts — robotic and virtual agents — at sponsored venues, with physiological sensing to measure moment-to-moment engagement and human-robot trust.

Why it matters to Coca-Cola

Turns sponsorship, including the FIFA World Cup 2026 footprint, from logo exposure into measured experience science.

Project D · Neurophysiological measurement

The experience signal.

What we build

An ethics-governed instrument measuring attention and engagement with products using EEG and physiological signals — neuromarketing moved to the point of choice.

Why it matters to Coca-Cola

An honest, independent read on what resonates — and a defensible framework for doing it responsibly.

Project E · Low-code BCI & robot authoring

NeuroBlock Studio.

What we build

NeuroBlock’s block-based paradigm rebuilt as an authoring toolkit, so experiential and agency teams compose brain- and robot-driven experiences without a neuroscientist in the room.

Why it matters to Coca-Cola

One-off activations become a reusable capability — faster ideation, lower cost, and a shared language between marketers and engineers.

Proven paradigm, new audience: the people who actually build your activations.

Project F · Service & assistive robotics

The serving arm.

What we build

A BCI-driven robotic pour-and-hand-off system: assistive for users who cannot operate a dispenser, hospitality robot for high-traffic venues.

Why it matters to Coca-Cola

A credible entry into service robotics for beverage, with the human-factors evidence most deployments skip.
