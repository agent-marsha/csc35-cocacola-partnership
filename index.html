Project A · Inclusive access

Every customer, every ability.

What we build

Prime III's universal-design method applied to next-gen dispensers — tactile, audio, haptic and multilingual interaction, usable by low-vision, blind, motor-impaired and first-time users without a phone.

Why it matters to Coca-Cola

Inclusive-by-design equipment widens the addressable market, answers tightening law, and turns "accessible" from a compliance cost into a brand strength.

Project B · Personalization

The responsive pour.

What we build

Dispensing that senses engagement and arousal — never identity — and adapts the Share-a-Coke moment in real time, inside a consent-first, privacy-preserving architecture.

Why it matters

Personalization that respects the person: higher experiential resonance, without the backlash of covert sensing.

Project C · Responsible AI

Fair by design.

What we build

Applications Quest adapted into an independent bias-audit framework for personalization, pricing and marketing models — measured across demographics, regions and store types, with reproducible metrics.

Why it matters

Credible, publishable assurance that a $1.1B AI investment treats consumers equitably — and a method that catches bias before it becomes a headline.

Project D · Sustainability

Every bottle back.

What we build

Behavioral science and culturally relevant design layered onto reverse vending — tested on the existing footprint, including units already running on the University of Alabama campus.

Why it matters

World Without Waste needs behavior change, not just machines. We test what moves people, community by community, and hand back a playbook.

Project E · Learning and workforce

The flavor academy.

What we build

Two learning platforms from one engine — an intelligent-tutoring system for bottler and franchise teams, and a consumer-facing "science of taste" experience — both authored with the low-code model proven by NeuroBlock.

Why it matters to Coca-Cola

Faster, cheaper workforce readiness across the bottling system, plus a branded learning experience that deepens consumer connection and feeds a STEM pipeline.

Project F · Brand experience

The living lab.

What we build

An instrumented experience at Coca-Cola-sponsored venues — real-time engagement measured with physiological sensing, plus embodied hosts for accessible hospitality and wayfinding.

Why it matters

Sponsorship becomes measured experience science, with findings that feed straight back into products.
